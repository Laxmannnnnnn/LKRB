# College Helper App

A centralized academic management platform that helps students organize their schedules, track academic activities, and access important college information in one place.

The College Helper App is designed to simplify student life by providing easy access to class schedules, timings, and essential updates. It reduces confusion, saves time, and improves productivity by replacing scattered sources of information with a single, user-friendly system.

---

## Project Overview

Students often struggle to manage their academic schedules, assignments, and important announcements due to the lack of a unified system. Information is usually spread across notice boards, messaging apps, and verbal communication.

The College Helper App solves this problem by offering a digital platform where students can view schedules, track activities, and stay updated with college-related information efficiently.

This project is being developed as part of a Capstone Design Project and demonstrates how web technologies can be used to improve academic organization and student productivity.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of the College Helper App is to create a system that:

- organizes academic schedules in one place  
- improves time management for students  
- reduces confusion caused by scattered information  
- provides quick and easy access to important updates  
- enhances productivity through a structured platform  

---

## Problem Statement

In many colleges, students face difficulties managing their academic responsibilities due to:

- lack of a centralized information system  
- missed classes due to unclear schedules  
- difficulty tracking assignments and deadlines  
- scattered announcements across different platforms  
- inefficient communication of important updates  

These issues can lead to reduced productivity and increased stress among students.

The College Helper App aims to solve these problems by providing a simple and unified academic management platform.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first working version of the College Helper App will include:

- View class schedules  
- Display daily and weekly timetables  
- Access basic academic information  
- Simple and user-friendly interface  

The MVP focuses on delivering core scheduling functionality in a clean and demo-ready format.

## Demo Scenario

The final demonstration will show a complete workflow:

- A student opens the College Helper App  
- The student views their class schedule  
- The student checks upcoming classes for the day  
- The student accesses important academic information  
- The student stays updated without checking multiple sources  

This demonstrates how the app simplifies academic management.
